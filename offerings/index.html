Work with Caerjar

We help people develop the tools and technical capacity their work requires. An engagement can begin with a question, an existing system, or a small working example.

Build software or a workflow

For a startup, research group, archive, or organization with a specific problem to solve.

We examine a representative sample of the work, identify the task that matters, and develop a prototype around it. The scope can include custom software, AI agent workflows, extraction, search, evidence review, authoring, conversion, or connections between existing systems.

Possible deliverables

A working prototype, documented inputs and outputs, checks for important failure cases, and a plan for maintenance or further development.

Related work

Corpusmith, Specsmith, Research-manager

Plan or assess a technical system

We bring technical depth and independent judgment to founders and organizations building things we believe in, from early architecture to the responsible use of AI.

We begin by understanding what you're trying to build and the values that guide it. We prioritize decentralized, private, and secure technologies, making the trade-offs clear so you can make informed decisions about the systems you depend on.

Possible deliverables

A technical assessment, a source-linked specification, a comparison of options, or an implementation and handoff plan.

Related work

Specsmith, Caer-stack

Develop local and shared infrastructure

For startups and groups that need services they can run and govern themselves.

We help define what belongs on the local system, how people gain access, where information is kept, and how another person can operate and restore it.

Possible deliverables

A configured prototype, deployment documentation, backup and recovery procedures, and an operator handoff.

Related work

Caer-stack, Local models

Learn by building

Workshops and individual tutoring for founders, researchers, artists, and organizations developing tools around their own work. Sessions can take place online or in person, individually or as a series.

Topics can include describing a useful small application, working with AI agent workflows, inspecting generated code, using sample data, and testing a tool before relying on it.

Possible deliverables

A small application or prototype, exercises and reference materials, and a clear account of the next steps.

Related work

Fieldkit, Training

Rates and scope

Rates on request. Community groups and mutual aid projects pay on a sliding scale. Scope and deliverables are agreed before work begins.
