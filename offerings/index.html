Work with Caerjar

We help people build the software, systems, and technical capacity their work requires. An engagement can begin with a question, an existing system, or a small working example.

Build custom software and tools

For early-stage ventures on short timelines, and for researchers, archives, and organizations with a specific problem to solve.

We begin with the real material and the task that matters, and make something small enough to test, question, and change. From there we build for real-world use, with attention to privacy, maintainability, and long-term ownership. The scope can include a first product for an early-stage venture, internal tools, AI systems and agent workflows, search and evidence review, or connections between existing systems.

Possible deliverables

Working software and its source code, documented inputs and outputs, checks for important failure cases, and a plan for maintenance or further development.

Related work

Corpusmith, Specsmith, Research-manager

Plan or assess a technical system

We bring technical depth and independent judgment to founders and organizations building things we believe in, from early architecture to the responsible use of AI.

We begin by understanding what you're trying to build and the values that guide it. We prioritize decentralized, private, and secure technologies, making the trade-offs clear so you can make informed decisions about the systems you depend on.

Possible deliverables

A technical assessment, a source-linked specification, a comparison of options, or an implementation and handoff plan.

Related work

Specsmith, Caer-stack

Develop independent infrastructure

For founders and communities that need services they can run and govern themselves.

We design and set up systems you control. Together we define what belongs on them, who has access, where information is kept, and how another person can operate and restore them.

Possible deliverables

A configured prototype, deployment documentation, backup and recovery procedures, and an operator handoff.

Related work

Caer-stack, Local models

Learn by building

Workshops and individual instruction for founders, researchers, artists, and communities. Sessions can take place online or in person, individually or as a series.

Participants develop working tools for their own creative, professional, or community practices, while learning to question and evaluate the systems they use. Topics can include describing a useful small application, working with AI agent workflows, inspecting generated code, using sample data, and testing a tool before relying on it.

Possible deliverables

A small application or prototype, exercises and reference materials, and a clear account of the next steps.

Related work

Fieldkit, Training

Rates and scope

Rates on request. Community groups and mutual aid projects pay on a sliding scale. Scope and deliverables are agreed before work begins.
