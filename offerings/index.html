What we do

We help our clients own the tools they depend on. Caerjar teaches, builds and advises on ethical AI and decentralized systems, and most projects use more than one of the three.

Teach

Learn to build your own tools with AI, and to do it with care. No programming background needed.

This can include

  • Agentic coding workshops. Sometimes called vibe coding. Everyone builds a working tool by describing it to an AI, then learns to read, test and fix what it made.
  • 1:1 tutoring. Sessions shaped around a tool you want to build or a skill you want to learn.
  • Privacy and security, built in. Every session covers where your data goes, how to keep what you build secure, which tools to trust, and when not to use AI at all.
  • Tools you can run yourselves. Where we can, we teach with open and local models, so what you learn doesn't depend on one company.

How it runs

Online or in person. A single session, or a series over several weeks that takes one tool from idea to finished.

You leave with

A tool you built yourself, and what you need to build the next one.

Shown in

  • Training A beginner's course in building small apps by describing them to a coding assistant

Build

Custom tools built with AI for the work you actually do, on systems you control. Privacy and security come first in every design, and nothing depends on one company's servers. We get a working version to you in days, then build the real thing so your team can run it without us.

This can include

  • Custom tools. Small, specific software for one job your team does every day.
  • AI agent workflows. Agents that do a defined task inside your work, with a person checking the results.
  • Local AI. Models that run on your own machines, so your data stays with you.
  • Decentralized systems. Self-hosted servers, mesh networks and local-first software a group runs itself.
  • Archives. A collection or an organization's memory, organized so it can be found and kept.

You leave with

Running software, its source code, and the notes whoever maintains it will need.

Advise

For organizations deciding how to use AI, or whether to. We look at what you need, what it will cost to keep running, and what it means for your privacy and your people. We start from the decentralized, private and secure options, and say plainly what each alternative would cost you.

This can include

  • Choosing AI ethically. Which models and vendors to trust, what they do with your data, and what you can run yourselves.
  • Guidelines for your team. What people can use AI for, how to check its work, and what stays off limits.
  • Privacy and security. Where your data goes, who can reach it, and what to change first.
  • Research and evidence review. Close reading of papers, sources and claims, with every judgement traceable.

You leave with

Our advice in writing, with the sources behind each part of it.

  • The Aion Apparatus An instrument for examining the ethics of made things
  • Ethical Imagination Trust, authorship and ethics, written into code
