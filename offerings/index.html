OFFERINGS

We help our clients own the tools they depend on. Three ways to work with us.

01

Advise

Counsel for organizations deciding what to build, adopt or leave alone.

Includes
  • Advisory. Whether to build, buy, adopt or leave a technology alone, and what it will cost to keep.
  • Research and evidence review. Close reading of papers, sources and claims, with every judgement traceable.
  • Ethics and governance design. How a group decides, who owns what, and how value is counted.
You leave with
A written recommendation, with the sources behind each part of it.
Shown in
  • Vetiver Examines a medical paper for validity and bias, and shows where every judgement came from
  • research-manager A Mac app for deciding what each research thread needs next
  • The Aion Apparatus An instrument for examining the ethics of made things
  • Ethical Imagination Trust, authorship and ethics, written into code
  • Unpaid Labor Coin Denominated in the work nobody counted
02

Build

A working version in days, to test an idea with the people it is for. Then the systems a group runs and owns itself.

Includes
  • Rapid prototyping. A working version in days, put in front of the people it is for.
  • Community infrastructure. Servers, networks and local-first software a group runs itself.
  • Archives and knowledge systems. Organizing a collection or an organization's memory so it can be found and kept.
You leave with
Running software, its source, and the notes whoever maintains it will need.
Shown in
  • Earshot A speech studio that runs on your own machine
  • Haunted Geography Memorial plaques you meet by standing where it happened
  • caer-stack Infrastructure a community runs for itself
  • Meridian A personal knowledge OS
  • Corpusmith Files a folder tree through a plan you review, and can move it all back
  • daimon A coding agent that runs entirely on your own machine
03

Teach

Courses, syllabi and workshops that teach people to build and keep their own tools.

Includes
  • Curriculum development. Courses and syllabi, written for the people who will take them.
  • Workshops and facilitation. Sessions where a group learns a practice by doing it.
You leave with
Course materials you can teach again without us.
Shown in
  • Training A beginner's course in building small apps by describing them to a coding assistant
Rates

Rates on request. Community groups and mutual aid projects pay on a sliding scale.

Tell us what you're tending and which of these fits. We'll write back.
