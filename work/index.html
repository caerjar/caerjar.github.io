WORK

Six terrains, and the work in each. Every piece links to its own page.

Terrains

01

Intelligence

Local agents, decision support, learning systems, and the preservation of human judgment.

  • daimon

    A coding agent that runs entirely on your own machine

  • research-manager

    A Mac app for deciding what each research thread needs next

  • The Aion Apparatus

    An instrument for examining the ethics of made things

02

Networks

Radio, mesh, media, coordination, and the movement of information across communities.

  • caer-stack

    Infrastructure a community runs for itself

  • Haunted Geography

    Memorial plaques you meet by standing where it happened

03

Archives

Archives, organizational memory, retrieval, documentation, oral history, and collective learning.

  • Meridian

    A personal knowledge OS

  • Corpusmith

    Files a folder tree through a plan you review, and can move it all back

04

Tools

Local-first software, electronics, sensing, resilient stacks, and physical systems that support collective life.

  • Earshot

    A speech studio that runs on your own machine

  • Training

    A beginner's course in building small apps by describing them to a coding assistant

  • epub-to-pdf

    Turn an e-book into a PDF for printing, with one fixed set of settings

  • Screenplay Formatter

    Turns a screenplay typed as plain text into a properly laid-out Word file

05

Medicine

Herbal practice, biological systems, ecology, environmental observation, community health, and care work.

  • Mugworts Free Herbal Clinic

    Care as collective infrastructure

  • Vetiver

    Examines a medical paper for validity and bias, and shows where every judgement came from

  • Drone Cinematographer

    A small drone that plans and flies documentary shots of plants without harming them

06

Stewardship

Collective action, institutions, resource allocation, ritual, accountability, and decision-making.

  • Ethical Imagination

    Trust, authorship and ethics, written into code

  • Unpaid Labor Coin

    Denominated in the work nobody counted

  • Peat and Repeat

    An artist-run edition house in Ridgewood

  • The Surregional Sangha Manifesto

    A speculative praxis for an anarchic spiritual community

Working on an archive, tool, medicine, network, or system you're tending? We'd like to hear about it.
