Caerjar exists to build technology that expands human and collective autonomy.

We design systems, develop software, and advise organizations working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, infrastructure, culture, and social transformation.

Our work combines deep technical expertise with a commitment to care, resilience, and self-determination. We help organizations move from ideas to working systems, building technological capacity rather than dependence.

We believe technology should serve the people and communities who use it, rather than extract value from them.

Three pillars of the practice

  1. Technology as a means of autonomy

    Developing decentralized, resilient, and community-controlled infrastructure, including local AI, archival systems, and tools that preserve institutional and collective knowledge.

  2. Engineering as a creative and intellectual practice

    Bringing together software architecture, artificial intelligence, critical inquiry, and artistic experimentation. Technology is not merely instrumental; it is also a medium through which we think, create, and relate.

  3. Building lasting technological capacity

    Helping organizations develop the technical strategy, systems, teams, and expertise to govern their own technological futures.
