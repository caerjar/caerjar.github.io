We build technology that expands what people and communities can do for themselves.

Caerjar designs and builds software, AI systems, and independent infrastructure. We advise organizations on consequential technical decisions and teach people to develop their own tools.

Our work is guided by a simple principle: the people who depend on technology should be able to understand it, shape it, and remain in control of it.

We work with founders, researchers, artists, and communities building things that matter.

What we do

Build

We design and develop custom software, AI systems, and independent infrastructure. From early prototypes to production systems, we build for real-world use, with attention to privacy, maintainability, and long-term ownership.

Advise

We provide independent technical judgment to founders and organizations navigating complex decisions. From architecture and engineering strategy to AI adoption, we help distinguish what is possible, what is useful, and what is worth building.

Teach

We teach people to build and understand technology for themselves. Through workshops and individual instruction, participants develop working tools for their own creative, professional, or community practices, while learning to question and evaluate the systems they use.

How we work

We begin by understanding the people, practices, and existing systems involved. Before building at scale, we make something small enough to test, question, and change.

We work in visible, reviewable stages, making technical decisions and tradeoffs explicit. We design for privacy, security, and maintainability from the beginning, and document what we build so others can understand and operate it.

Good technology should increase people's capacity to act, not create new forms of dependence.
