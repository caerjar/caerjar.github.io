Caerjar's mission is to build technology that expands human and collective autonomy.

We build research tools and community infrastructure, advise on technical decisions, and teach people to develop software around their own work. We help our clients own the tools they depend on.

We work with startups, research groups, cultural organizations, and communities that want to understand, maintain, and govern their systems.

What we do

Build

Custom software, AI agent workflows, local models, and decentralized systems, for startups, research groups, archives, and communities. We take a specific problem from a working example through implementation, documentation, and handoff.

Advise

We bring technical depth and independent judgment to founders and organizations building things we believe in, from early architecture to the responsible use of AI.

Teach

Practical workshops and individual tutoring in building software, including AI agent workflows. Participants work on a problem of their own, learning how to inspect what a tool does and recognize its limits.

How we work

We begin with the material and the people who will use the result. A small example helps establish what the system needs to do and what it must leave alone.

The work proceeds through reviewable steps. We make assumptions explicit, test the important boundaries, and document how the result can be operated and maintained. Decisions about access, publication, and sensitive material remain with the people responsible for them.
