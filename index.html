A small technology studio. We help our clients own the tools they depend on.

Most organizations rent their software and hope it keeps working. We help you choose, build and run tools your own people can understand and change: an archive that stays yours, an AI model that runs on your own machines and keeps your data there, a network your group runs itself.

We care about privacy, security and the ethics of what gets built, and we say so plainly when the right answer is not to build anything.

What we do

Advise

Before you build or buy, we look at what you need, what it will cost to keep running, and what it means for your privacy and your people. You get our advice in writing, with the sources behind it.

Build

We get a working version in front of the people who will use it in days, not months. Then we build the real thing, including AI agent workflows, so your team can run it without us.

Teach

Workshops and courses for teams who want to build and look after their own tools, including how to work well with AI coding assistants.
