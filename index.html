A small technology studio. We help our clients own the tools they depend on.

Caerjar teaches, builds and advises on ethical AI and decentralized systems. Most organizations rent their software and hope it keeps working. We help you build tools with AI that your own people can understand, run and change, on systems you control.

We care about privacy, security and the ethics of what gets built, and we say so plainly when the right answer is not to build anything.

What we do

Teach

Agentic coding workshops, sometimes called vibe coding: you build a real tool by describing it to an AI, and learn to check what it makes. For groups or one to one, online or in person, as a single session or a series over several weeks.

Build

Custom tools built with AI for your work, and decentralized systems your group runs itself, from self-hosted servers and local AI to mesh networks. We get a working version to you in days, and leave it so your team can run it without us.

Advise

Using AI ethically: which models to trust, where your data goes, what to run on your own machines, and the guidelines your team needs. You get our advice in writing, with the sources behind it.
